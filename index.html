Purpose

Our purpose is to share and preserve the cultural richness of our region, using the digital environment as a bridge between traditions and new generations. We believe that culture is a living treasure that grows stronger when shared, and that everyone, regardless of origin, can learn, be inspired, and keep the flame of our identity alive. By knowing our roots, we discover our future.

Mission

Create an interactive digital space that promotes Peruvian gastronomy, music, art, native languages, and customs, bringing these cultural expressions closer to young people and adults in an attractive and participatory way.

Vision

To be the most recognized cultural platform in Peru, a benchmark in the creative dissemination of our roots and a bridge between generations, communities, and the world.

Values

  1. Respect for diversity and traditions.
  2. Authenticity in every story and content.
  3. Inclusion of all voices and regions.
  4. Creativity to showcase culture in innovative ways.
  5. Collaboration among communities, artists, and the public.

Extraordinary Activities!

In-person and virtual workshops on regional gastronomy that combine the preparation of typical dishes and their cultural history.

Educational program to connect students with indigenous communities and preserve ancestral knowledge such as music and weaving.

Participatory muralism initiatives in neighborhoods and rural communities that capture local narratives.
